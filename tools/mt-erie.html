Mt. Erie

Anacortes / Fidalgo Island · 1,270ft elevation · Granite

Fetching conditions…

Drying Speed
Very Slow (island climate)

Granite dries fast. Lower Wall sheds water quickly; Upper Wall slower after heavy rain. South-facing aspect means afternoon sun helps dry things out. One of the most reliable crags on the west side when conditions cooperate.
