Tree

SRE · Platform Engineer · Cascade Climber

I'm a Site Reliability Engineer with 10+ years building production infrastructure on AWS, based in Seattle and looking for roles at the intersection of technical depth and things that actually matter — climate, sustainability, outdoor access, and the communities built around them.

I'm also co-facilitator of Rising Roots, a climbing community creating welcoming spaces for BIPOC climbers in the PNW. I built BETA because a tool like this didn't exist for the crags we climb — and because production-grade engineering is exactly what a community resource deserves.

When I'm not thinking about infrastructure, I'm at Seattle Bouldering Project, riding with Northstar Cycling, or planning my next Cascades objective. Patagonia evangelist. Data nerd. Mountain person.
